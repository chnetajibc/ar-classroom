## Long description

```
CLASSROOM INTERIOR PACK - TEACHER & STUDENTS (URP)

A ready-to-run classroom for Unity 6 and the Universal Render Pipeline. Open the demo scene and press Play: the teacher walks to the blackboard, writes a data-structures lesson in chalk, turns to the class, explains it and asks a question. Nine seated students follow her with head and eyes, three raise their hands and one answers. Click a student to make the teacher call on them; click the board for the next lesson.

Use it as a finished demo, as a starting point for e-learning, training or visualisation projects, or as a kit: every wall, desk, prop and character is a separate prefab at real-world scale (1 unit = 1 m).

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Demo scene and a "Classroom_Complete" prefab: 7.5 x 7.5 x 3.2 m room with blackboard, nine student desks and chairs, teacher's desk and armchair, windows, door, ceiling fans, fluorescent lights, shelves, plants, and a laptop, books and pens on every student desk
- 7 modular architecture prefabs (wall, window wall, doorway wall, door, floor, ceiling, blackboard) that tile without seams
- 7 furniture prefabs and 18 prop and lighting prefabs
- 10 rigged Humanoid characters (1 teacher, 9 students) and the Universal Animation Library (about 86 Humanoid clips; the demo uses 9)
- Lesson system: 7 data-structures lessons (Arrays, Linked List, Stack & Queue, Big-O, Binary Search Tree, quick reference, motto) stored as plain data; replace them with your own from the Inspector or from code
- Camera rig with 6 view presets, orbit and zoom, on-screen captions
- Head and eye look-at, hand-raise and chalk-writing IK; seated feet pinned to the floor
- Works with the legacy Input Manager and with the Input System package
- One-click editor tools: "Apply Recommended URP Settings" and "Rebuild Demo Scene"
- PDF documentation, changelog and third-party notices

TECHNICAL DETAILS
- Unity 6 (6000.x); developed and tested on 6000.6.1f1 with URP 17.6 on macOS (Apple silicon)
- Universal Render Pipeline only (not Built-in, not HDRP); Linear colour space recommended
- 42 prefabs + 1 complete-room prefab; 42 FBX models + 2 animation FBX files
- Characters: 1,786 to 6,574 triangles each, Humanoid avatars, 1.70 m tall
- 126 URP/Lit materials; 89 textures (13 at 2048 px, 49 at 1024 px, 26 at 512 px, 1 at 128 px) plus one 2048x1024 HDRI
- Demo scene: about 629,000 triangles, 195 renderers, 11 lights, real-time lighting (Forward+, SSAO, ACES tonemapping, bloom)
- 14 C# scripts in the RealisticClassroom namespace with 3 assembly definitions; no plugins or extra dependencies (URP and uGUI ship with Unity)
- Supports Fast Enter Play Mode (Domain Reload disabled)
- About 61 MB download, 106 MB imported

GOOD TO KNOW (limitations)
- The characters are stylised, low-poly and flat-shaded, not photorealistic. There are 10 distinct characters; students share two sitting animations with random offsets.
- No lip-sync, facial animation or audio. Captions are on-screen text.
- URP only. Tested on one Unity version (6000.6.1f1) and one platform (macOS Editor); not tested on mobile, WebGL, XR or consoles.
- The demo is meant for desktop: one plant is about 119,000 triangles, and there are no LODs or baked lighting.

THIRD-PARTY CONTENT
All third-party models, textures, the HDRI and the animations are CC0 (public domain), from Poly Haven, Kenney, Quaternius and other CC0 sources. Nothing requires attribution; authors and links are listed in "Third-Party Notices.txt" inside the package. The scripts, editor tools, prefab set-up, demo scene and documentation are the publisher's own work.

AI DISCLOSURE
The C# scripts, editor tools (including the script that assembles the demo scene) and the documentation were written with an AI coding assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) under the publisher's direction and tested in the Unity Editor. No 3D models, textures, animations, audio or images in this package were generated by AI.
```
